Notice of changes to opening hours

From next month, the customer service desk will be open from Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm. On Thursdays it will stay open until 7 pm. Applications can still be submitted online at any time.

Please remember to book an appointment before visiting. Documents must be signed and, where required, accompanied by proof of payment of the relevant fee.

The telephone information line will keep its usual hours. Thank you for your understanding.
