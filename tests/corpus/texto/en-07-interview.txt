Interview notes, community support programme

Participant: Aisha Rahman, 41, lives in Bradford with her two children.

Aisha is a practising Muslim and wears the hijab. She describes herself as British Pakistani. She has been a member of the Labour Party for twelve years and is an active member of the Unite union, where she represents warehouse staff.

She mentioned that her brother, Imran Rahman, is gay and moved to Leeds last year. She is currently being treated for anxiety.

Notes taken by: Gareth Hughes, outreach coordinator.
